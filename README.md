An original browser card duel: you are Bram, the Hearth Warden, facing Vexa, the Ashen Queen. Play kindreds (minions with attack, health and the Guard, Swift or Burn keywords) and rites (sears, mends, blesses and draws) from a growing mana pool, trade in the lanes, and break the enemy queen's 25 hearts before she breaks yours. Every card, name, flavour line and colour composition is original to Emberhold; no libraries, no build step, no network requests.

Vexa is a readable opponent: she takes favourable trades, spends her rites where they finish a minion or protect her line, and goes face when the lane is clear — so you can read her plan and counter it.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts          # serves game/ on http://localhost:8080
```

or hand it in for grading:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
